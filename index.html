Section 01

The case in 30 seconds.

We are great and we are capped. The team produces more than most marketing orgs twice our size. We are also at the edge of capacity, and we lose momentum at every cross-functional handoff. Sales does not know what we shipped. Product tells us about launches late. DG leads sit waiting for the BDR. Post-event lead processing is manual.

Why now: two windows. Pre-AEP starts in July. Recruiting season starts in February. If we have an agent fleet running by July, AEP 2027 is the first AEP we run proactively. We get a moat that competitors cannot easily copy, and our "AI-first broker model" positioning gets to point at something real.

Side benefit: the same infrastructure gives every team member personal leverage. The Context layer, skills library, and Claude access mean better 1:1 prep, faster meeting follow-up, tighter task organization. Personal productivity is not a strategic pillar of this plan, but it comes free with the build.

Section 02

The framework: an agentic marketing org.

Four layers. Strategy flows down. Execution flows up. Results flow back into the foundation.

CONTEXT · FOUNDATION

The knowledge layer everything reads from.

Spark voice, ICP personas, market intelligence, performance memory, product context. Built first. Sets the ceiling on how good every agent above can be. Detail in Section 04.

EXECUTION · WHERE WORK HAPPENS

Three agent clusters running in parallel.

Content engine, demand gen fleet, marketing ops fleet. Each agent owns a repeatable workstream end-to-end. Craft specialists supervise and steer. Detail in Section 03.

ORCHESTRATION · TEAM RHYTHM

Same people. New operating rhythm.

Not a new role. The existing team's planning cadence becomes how the fleet stays aimed. Three rhythms: Weekly Fleet Review (what agents shipped, what to tune), Performance + Playbook Loop (results back into Context), Status + Reporting (internal updates and exec visibility).

LEADERSHIP · STRATEGY + JUDGMENT

The layer that stays human.

Direction-setting, AEP narrative, exec presence, board comms, talent, partner relationships. Feeds strategy back into Context so agents stay aimed at the right outcomes.

Section 03

The agent fleet.

Three clusters. Content, Demand Gen, Marketing Ops. Phase 1 (green) is in production by July for pre-AEP. Phase 2 (purple) is ready by February for next recruiting season.

Content Engine
5 agents
1 Newsletter agent
Drafts the 5 monthly newsletters (Spark agent, AP, IMN EN/ES, prospect) and the weekly AEP newsletter from the topic calendar. Routes SME reviews via Slack approvals.
1 Webinar agent
Triggers on "webinar created in Asana." Drafts deck, Luma event, Beamer post, 2 promo emails, social posts, ads, recap email. Handles SFDC tagging and BDR routing post-event.
1 Thought Leadership agent
Indexes Granola transcripts, internal POVs, CMS news. Drafts James and Spark LinkedIn posts in voice. Captures news-cycle moments we currently miss.
2 Evangelist Blog agent
Turns Jesse, Nick, Liz, and Pam's POVs from Granola + Slack into publish-ready blog posts. Recruiting-season content engine.
2 Repurposing agent
Takes one webinar and emits 5 social posts + 1 blog + 1 newsletter blurb + Help Center update. Multiplies output from existing assets.
Demand Gen Fleet
7 agents
1 MQL Scoring agent
Every inbound lead gets scored against our ICP, intent signals, firmographic fit, and engagement history. Routes high-fit leads to the BDR Brief agent immediately. Sends low-fit leads to the Nurture Sequencer. Replaces our current "all MQLs look the same" handoff.
1 BDR Brief + Lead Routing agent
Triggered on a qualified lead from the MQL Scoring agent. Enriches via Apollo/Clay, checks SFDC for existing relationship, drafts a 1-page prospect brief, routes to BDR/AE by territory, enforces 6-min response SLA, escalates stalled leads to Slack.
2 MQL Nurture Sequencer
When a new offer is downloaded (Agent Needs report, Agency Assessment, Recruiting Checklist), picks the right nurture sequence, drafts emails in voice, schedules in HubSpot. Handles re-engagement for cold leads too.
2 Self-Serve Segmentation agent
Natural language to list. "All field APs in TX with >50 enrollments who haven't engaged in 60 days" returns a HubSpot segment. Eliminates manual data pulls from Liz.
2 Pipeline Diagnostic + DG Reporter
Drafts Diana's weekly DG update from SFDC + HubSpot + paid platforms. Flags where leads stall, surfaces conversion drops by source, recommends interventions.
2 Account Intel agent
When an AE is assigned a new account, generates a one-pager: org structure, decision-makers, recent moves (LinkedIn), pain signals, prior touchpoint history from Granola.
2 Paid Creative + Offer agent
Generates new FB/LI/Google ad variants from top performers. Builds offer landing pages from briefs. Compresses Diana's vibe-coded one-off workflow into a reusable pipeline.
Marketing Ops Fleet
5 agents
2 SFDC Hygiene + Attribution agent
Monitors campaign tagging, opp creation timing, attribution chains. Auto-creates opps for engaged prospects. Fixes the chronic SFDC attribution gap that broke during Q1 rollout.
2 Sales Enablement / Asset Library agent
Sellers ask "do we have a Spark intro deck?" in Slack. Agent finds it, returns the right version, logs the request to inform what to build. Closes the chronic Marketing-Sales gap.
2 AEP Self-Serve Knowledge Base
The Q&A bot for sellers, AMs, and (eventually) agents. Indexed across AEP Notion, comms log, training materials. Fixes the 2025 retro's #1 gap: no single source of AEP truth.
2 Post-Event Lead Processor
Triggered on event close. Pulls badge scans + Luma + booth scans, enriches, dedupes against SFDC, tags campaign, scores, routes to AE/AM by territory. Medicarians took 3 hours manually; this is the agent that ends that.
2 Status + Reporting agent
Drafts the weekly marketing Slack update, monthly leadership review, and QBR materials by pulling from performance memory + agent outputs. Team owns the analysis and storytelling. Agent removes the drafting time sink. Powers the Orchestration layer's reporting rhythm.
