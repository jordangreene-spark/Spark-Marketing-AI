Section 01

Why?

We're doing great work, but spread thin and capped on output. The team produces similar to marketing orgs twice our size. We are also at the edge of capacity, and we lose momentum at every cross-functional handoff. We need less manual, high calorie work and more systems that support us scaling so humans can focus on programs that require high quality.

We are already actively using Claude. We're already highly active with Claude for personal productivity & some offers (Agency Assessment, ROI Calculator, SNP growth maps). What we have not done: build the Context and Orchestration layers so agents do meaningful work independently and at scale.

Why now: We believe pre-AEP is the best time for us to engage the largest number of agents & APs due to their need to get ready for AEP. This is a massive opportunity for Spark, but it requires scale & efficiency, which we can only achieve with AI.

Section 02

The framework: an agentic marketing org.

Four distinct layers that make it possible for agents to operate.

CONTEXT · FOUNDATION

The knowledge layer everything reads from.

Spark voice, ICP personas, market intelligence, performance memory, product context. Built first. Sets the ceiling on how good every agent above can be. Detail in Section 04.

EXECUTION · WHERE WORK HAPPENS

Three agent clusters running in parallel.

Content engine, demand gen fleet, marketing ops fleet. Each agent owns a repeatable workstream end-to-end. Craft specialists supervise and steer. Detail in Section 03.

ORCHESTRATION · TEAM RHYTHM

Same people. New operating rhythm.

Not a new role. The existing team's planning cadence becomes how the fleet stays aimed. Three rhythms: Weekly Fleet Review (what agents shipped, what to tune), Performance + Playbook Loop (results back into Context), Status + Reporting (internal updates and exec visibility).

LEADERSHIP · STRATEGY + JUDGMENT

The layer that stays human.

Direction-setting, AEP narrative, exec presence, board comms, talent, partner relationships. Feeds strategy back into Context so agents stay aimed at the right outcomes.

When a new offer is downloaded (Agent Needs report, Agency Assessment, Recruiting Checklist), picks the right nurture sequence, drafts emails in voice, schedules in HubSpot. Handles re-engagement for cold leads too.

Natural language to list. "All field APs in TX with >50 enrollments who haven't engaged in 60 days" returns a HubSpot segment. Eliminates manual data pulls from Liz.
3 Pipeline Diagnostic + DG Reporter
Drafts Diana's weekly DG update from SFDC + HubSpot + paid platforms. Flags where leads stall, surfaces conversion drops by source, recommends interventions.

When an AE is assigned a new account, generates a one-pager: org structure, decision-makers, recent moves (LinkedIn), pain signals, prior touchpoint history from Granola.
3 Paid Creative + Offer agent
Generates new FB/LI/Google ad variants from top performers. Builds offer landing pages from briefs. Compresses Diana's vibe-coded one-off workflow into a reusable pipeline.

Triggered on event close. Pulls badge scans + Luma + booth scans, enriches, dedupes against SFDC, tags campaign, scores, routes to AE/AM by territory. Recruiting events (NABIP, AAC, regional roadshows) generate the volume that demands this.

Section 05

Personal productivity for the team.

Personal productivity is a separate altitude from the marketing function. But the same infrastructure (Context, skills, Claude access) gives every team member real personal leverage. Here is the simple framework we enable everyone with.

Goal: every team member saves 5-8 hours a week on admin, planning, reporting, and status updates within 60 days. Modeled by Jordan + Diana from the start so the rest of the team has a pattern to copy.

The four pillars.

01

Set up personal context.

Connect calendar, Slack DMs, Granola, email, Asana. The same connectors the team fleet uses become each person's personal substrate. Everyone gets the same Claude desktop + Cowork setup.

02

Build a personal recipe book.

Save the prompts you use repeatedly. "Prep me for my 1:1 with Diana." "Summarize my action items from this week's Granola notes." "Draft my Friday update from my recent Asana activity."

03

Establish daily and weekly rhythms.

Morning brief: 10 minutes of Claude prep before the day starts. Friday wrap-up: 15-minute Claude-drafted Slack post. Monthly admin sweep: expenses, calendar cleanup, follow-up audit.

04

Share what works back to the team.

When Fiona builds a great prompt, everyone benefits. We codify the wins in the Personal Productivity Recipe Book inside the Team Operating System folder of the Knowledge Index.

Section 08

June AI Plan.

A 5-week sprint. No new headcount. The team builds together every Tuesday afternoon.

The mechanism

Tuesday afternoons become AI co-build sessions.

Every Tuesday in June, we block the second half of our no-meeting day for an AI co-build session. Four hours. Whole team in the same room (or Zoom). Diana leads.

The team co-owns the build. Everyone sees how every agent gets made. The agents stick because the people who supervise them helped build them.

Week 2 · Jun 9

Co-write the Webinar Playbook (12 steps), the 5 Newsletter Templates, MQL Scoring criteria, BDR Brief format, and Thought Leadership voice rules.
